1. Coin Toss Hypothesis Test

  • Given: n=900, α=0.05, p=0.5
  • Mean: μ=np=900×0.5=450
  • Std Dev: σ=np(1−p)​=900×0.5×0.5​=15
  • z-critical: zα/2​=1.96
  • Acceptance region:
    • 450±1.96(15)=[420.6,479.4]
  • Conclusion: Reject H0​ if heads ≤420 or ≥480

2. Guinea Pig Birth-Weight Test

  • Data: n=27, xˉ=325.4963 g, s=198.7855 g

(a) Hypothesis Test (α=0.05)

  • Null: H0​:μ=300
  • Test stat:
    • t=198.7855/27​325.4963−300​≈0.6665
  • df: n−1=26, t0.025,26​≈2.0555
  • Conclusion: ∣t∣<2.0555 → Fail to reject H0​

(b) Smallest α

  • p-value: p=2(1−Ft26​​(0.6665))≈0.5110
  • Conclusion: Only reject H0​ if α>0.511

(c) 95% Confidence Interval

  • xˉ±t0.025,26​⋅n​s​
  • 325.4963±2.0555⋅27​198.7855​
  • CI ≈(246.86,404.13) g
